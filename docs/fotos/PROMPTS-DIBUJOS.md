## Estilo (va dentro de cada prompt)

> Create a new exercise illustration that matches the attached example drawings EXACTLY in style. The examples come from the folder docs/fotos/referencias/ (real Everkinetic exercise drawings, black lines on white): copy their line weight, anatomy, proportions, head, shorts, feet and the way equipment is drawn, so the new image looks like it belongs to the same set. In short: black ink line art on a pure white background; one athletic, muscular adult man; bald, smooth head with NO facial features (no eyes, nose or mouth; only the ear outline); shirtless, wearing plain loose shorts with a waistband line; barefoot; clean, confident black contour lines of even thickness (like a 3 px pen) and thinner interior lines that only suggest the main muscles (chest, abs, deltoids, back, quads); NO shading, NO hatching, NO grey, NO colour, NO gradients, NO shadows, NO floor line, NO background objects; equipment drawn in the same simple outline style (barbell = straight bar with round plates; benches and machines = clean geometric outlines); three-quarter view (or a straight side view when it shows the movement better), the whole body and the equipment fully inside the frame with a generous white margin; anatomically correct, safe exercise technique; realistic hands and feet. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, arrows, logos or watermarks. Square 1024×1024 image.
