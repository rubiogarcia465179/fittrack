**Descripción** (1495/4000)
```
FitTrack is a gym app that does the thinking for you, so you can focus on lifting.

TODAY'S PLAN, DECIDED
Open the app and it tells you what to train today: chest and arms, back, legs… One tap to start, one tap to change it. Choose your time, intensity, energy and goal, and FitTrack builds the session.

EVERY SET IN TWO TAPS
Only the current exercise is on screen. Pick reps and weight with a wheel, like the iPhone alarm, and the set is done. The rest timer starts by itself and alerts you even with the phone locked.

PROGRESS YOU CAN TRUST
When you completed every rep last time, FitTrack suggests 2.5 kg more, or you keep the weight with one tap. New personal records light up green at the moment you lift them. Personal records and estimated 1RM come only from sets you actually did.

KNOW HOW TO DO IT
205 real exercises, each with three plain steps, a line drawing of the start and end position for the classics, and a short video that plays right inside the app.

YOUR TRAINING DIARY
Every session becomes a post with your sets, how it felt, your own photos and comments.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no sign-up, no cloud, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone. Save a full backup to a file (Files, iCloud Drive or a folder) and restore it on a new phone in seconds.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
English, Spanish, Dutch and French, switch instantly.

Credits: exercise database by wger.de (CC BY-SA). Exercise drawings by Everkinetic (CC BY-SA 3.0, modified). Videos from YouTube.
```
